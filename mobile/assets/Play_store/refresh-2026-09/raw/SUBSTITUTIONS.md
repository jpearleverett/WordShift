# Capture substitutions and deviations (refresh-2026-09)

Every capture in this folder is a genuine full-frame render of the current Expo web build (git HEAD `4fc0121f`; `s07`, `s08`, `t2-board` and `t3` were recaptured on the working tree that adds the tablet board and story-card fixes, committed together with this folder), made by `mobile/scripts/store/captureRefresh.mjs`. Nothing is cropped or resampled here. `provenance.json` lists, per file, the seeded state, viewport, DPR, reducedMotion, timestamp, SHA-256, the visible text and the measured crop window (CSS px and device px of the file).

The brief is followed everywhere except the points below. Each one keeps to the hard boundaries (real UI only, no DOM edits, no invented words, no spoilers) and says what the build step or the owner has to know.

## 1. The tablet board needed a game fix; the set is now board, day house, cup, pit

- **Brief:** `t2-board.png`, the opener board with the L lifted, at 720x1280, DPR 2.
- **Problem found:** at 600 CSS and wider the game scaled the whole board by up to 1.2 (`computeBoardScale`, `TABLET_MAX_SCALE` 1.2) as a bare `transform: scale`, while each row card was already as wide as the screen, so the PICK, DROP and third-row cards ran off both screen edges (measured at 720: row extents -67 to 787 CSS, -49 to 769 and -7 to 727) and the PICK and DROP tags were cut in half. A real defect on every 7-inch and 10-inch tablet in portrait.
- **Fix (game code, same change):** `getBoardScaleWrapperStyle` in `src/services/slotEstimation.ts` lays the rows out at `1/scale` of the width and reserves the height the scale adds, so after the scale the rows span exactly the board area. The board stays centred, so the drag math is unchanged.
- **Capture:** `captureRefresh.mjs t2wide` now writes `t2-board.png` and refuses to capture if any row content leaves the screen. It leads the tablet set (`upload/tablet/t1-board.png`), followed by the day house (`t2-house.png`, from raw `t1-house.png`), the cup page and the pit (`t4-pit.png`, from raw `t2-pit.png`). The dusk well (raw `t4-dusk-well.png`) stays in raw but is not uploaded: the phone set already carries the dusk house.
- **Owner:** upload the tablet set once a Play build carrying the fix is live.

## 7. Second review fixes (2026-09-23)

- **Drifting clouds.** The house's three drifting clouds sit at fixed heights (at the kitchen's top edge in slot 03, beside the study in slot 04) and, under reduced motion, rest exactly there, where the caption seam or the house column sliced them into grey blobs. Slots 03 and 04 and the two tablet house frames are therefore taken with motion on and Playwright's page clock installed: once the scene is staged, the script fast-forwards the game's clock in 0.9 s jumps until every cloud is wholly behind the house column or wholly clear of it (and, in slots 03 and 04, wholly outside the window), then pauses the clock there and takes the frame. Only time moves; nothing on the page is touched. `provenance.json` records the fast-forward (`clockFastForwardMs`) and every cloud box.
- **Dust motes and emotes.** With motion on the house also floats small warm dust motes and chimney smoke; the same pause waits until no mote and no emote puff sits on a room or name plaque (one first take had a mote on SCHOLAR'S STUDY).
- **Road seam.** Where the foundation's carved road meets the pit entrance art, a one-pixel dark line shows across the dirt path at some sub-pixel positions of the house (a gap between the two images). Each house frame that shows the well is scanned for it (`seamRows`: a row of the path darker than 0.86 of its neighbourhood); slot 03 is retaken at the next valid DPR and the tablet frames after a small real drag until the path is clean. The owner may also want to close the gap in the game (`carveFoundationRoad.mjs` or `PIT_MARGIN_TOP`).
- **Pan precision with motion on.** A drag's release carries momentum when motion is on, so the pan helper now calibrates each drag from the travel it actually produced and makes small corrections as an overshoot and a return.
- **State A** also seeds `completedDifficulties: ['EASY']`. It changes no still (no still shows state A's victory), but the trailer's opener win is then the true seventh-win card (EASY 8, three stars +4, no first-clear bonus), not a first EASY clear a player at six solves could not see.
