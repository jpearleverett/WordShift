One letter moves. Two words change. A little home grows.

WordShift is a word puzzle game with a warm woodland heart and a mystery that slowly stirs beneath it.

A small move. A satisfying discovery.

Take a letter from one word and place it in the next. Both words must stay real. Move the L from PLAY into PANT, and you get PAY and PLANT. Keep the chain going to solve the puzzle.

Easy to understand, with plenty to think about: a letter you move locks into its new word, so your next choice matters.

Build somewhere worth coming back to.

Earn amber by solving puzzles. Open rooms in a growing pixel-art house, add special room details, and welcome 13 animal companions. Get to know Ember the fox, Panko the pangolin, Axel the axolotl, and the rest of a very unusual household.

There are conversations by the fire, little routines, and familiar faces waiting when you return.

Stay for the mystery.

As the house grows, its warmth begins to feel different. Conversations change. Small details ask for a second look. Your choices become part of a story you can revisit in your journal.

The mystery unfolds through play. No purchase is needed to follow the main story.

Find your kind of challenge.

More styles and challenges open as you progress.

- Thousands of word puzzles across five difficulty levels
- Reverse Shift, which takes the chain down and back up
- Double Shift, with two letters to move at each step
- Optional timed Speed Shift and hidden-preview Blind Mode
- Lexicon mode for players who enjoy rarer words
- Practice boards to learn the twists
- Daily challenges, streaks, and daily and weekly quests
- 56 achievements to work toward
- Tile styles, room details, and a home that changes as you play

Make room for a little wordplay.

Core puzzles work offline, and no account is required. Use a hint when you need a nudge. Adjust sound, music, motion, and haptics to suit you. Online features and purchases require an internet connection.

Contains ads and in-app purchases, including an optional auto-renewing Supporter subscription. The story develops unsettling themes and mild horror.
