# WordShift Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** June 10, 2026

WordShift ("the app") is a word puzzle game developed by Jonathan Pearl Everett ("we", "us"). This policy describes what information the app handles and how.

## The short version

WordShift is designed to work entirely on your device. By default, **no personal information leaves your phone**. There are no accounts, no ads, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

## Information stored on your device

All gameplay data is stored locally on your device using your operating system's app storage:

- Puzzle progress, statistics, star ratings, and achievements
- In-game currency (amber), unlocks, and story progress
- Settings (sound, haptics, reduced motion, notification preferences)
- A locally generated random install identifier and a local event log used for debugging

This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app, or using **Settings → Reset All Progress**, permanently deletes this data.

## Notifications

If you choose to enable reminders, the app schedules **local notifications** on your device (a daily puzzle reminder and an occasional check-in nudge). These are generated on your device; no push-notification service or server is involved. You can disable them at any time in the app's Settings or your system settings.

## Diagnostics

The app contains an optional diagnostics component that is **switched off**: no network endpoint is configured and no data is transmitted off your device.

## Children's privacy

WordShift does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Note that the game's story gradually introduces dark-fantasy/horror themes intended for ages 12 and up.

## Data sharing and selling

We do not collect, share, or sell personal data. There is nothing to share or sell.

## Your choices

- **Reset everything:** Settings → Reset All Progress
- **Notifications:** Settings → Daily Reminders, or your system notification settings
