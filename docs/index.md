# WordShift

**Shift one letter. Keep both words real.**

Pick up the L in PLAY. Drop it into PANT. Now you have PAY and PLANT, and a fox
by the fire looks very pleased with you.

WordShift is a cozy word puzzle game built on one small move: take a letter
from one word and place it in the next, and both words must stay real. Every
puzzle you solve earns amber to build a pixel-art house, room by room, for 13
animal housemates who walk around their rooms, talk about their day and pick
up the conversation where you left off.

It's a cozy game. Mostly. The house keeps a few secrets.

## Features

- Over 4,000 word puzzles across five difficulty levels, from four-letter
  warmups to six-letter Expert chains
- Reverse Shift and Double Shift, plus four modifiers you can layer on any
  style: Challenge Mode, Speed Shift, Blind Mode and Lexicon for rarer words
- A daily word puzzle with streaks and a leaderboard
- 13 animal housemates, each with a room of their own and a story that unfolds
  in illustrated scenes
- Share your results with friends
- No energy meters or lives, and no account needed; cloud backups sync when
  you're online
