# September review implementation status — updated September 6

This ledger tracks the implementation following the [43-item review](GAME_REVIEW_AND_IMPROVEMENT_PLAN_2026-09-05.md). The review's opening statement and test totals describe the original read-only audit of `8297227`; they are not the delivery status below. Version 1.3.0 / Android 94 / iOS 3 is prepared in source, with Expo 57.0.17 and React Native 0.86.3. A new signed native binary is required. No signed build, store submission, production SQL deployment or reader pilot is claimed here.

**Implemented** means the described code is present, with the stated local checks. It does not waive the remaining acceptance checks. **Ready to execute** identifies prepared tooling or an operational procedure, not an observed production outcome. Engineering owns code/test follow-ups; the release owner owns signed-device and store checks; a backend operator owns hosted migrations/support/retention; the narrative owner owns unfamiliar-reader checks.

## Additional vocabulary integrity work

The [vocabulary audit](VOCABULARY_AUDIT_2026-09-05.md) and [editorial decisions](VOCABULARY_EDITORIAL_DECISIONS_2026-09-06.md) distinguish accepted historical words from fair required puzzle vocabulary. Ordinary routes use the reviewed common band; Expert and explicit Lexicon modes use their stated wider band. New entries require positive audited membership. The raw 22,749-word dictionary and saved-board compatibility remain intact.

Bank selection, fallback boards and generated/extended routes are checked before delivery. The six previously invalid emergency fallbacks were replaced in the first delivery, and all 60 fallback boards have a fair complete route. This follow-up also repaired advanced generator vocabulary context, reverse move/lock/remainder proofs and hint metadata, and blocked unsafe Double Shift intermediate strings. The official purge removed three affected live boards; a fourth old seed was caught and replaced during staging.

The [completed gated top-up](PUZZLE_BANK_TOP_UP_2026-09-06.md) delivers **4,372 qualified boards from 7,356 stored records**: **1,599 Standard**, **1,379 Reverse** and **1,394 Double Shift**. Thirteen thin pools were replenished; every pool now reaches 100 without relaxing rarity, word-cap, letter-monotony or complete-route requirements. Standard boards retain at least two routes, Reverse boards complete both legs, and Double Shift boards complete their paired moves. Independent audits replay canonical solutions and preferred hints and find no delivered duplicates or hygiene failures. Stored bank source shrank by 2,806,989 bytes while eligible capacity rose by 529 from the post-editorial baseline.

The content-derived daily cohort is `daily_v2_e8f99141efc9d9c6`; saved daily attempts retain their recorded cohort. Native generation latency, rare-word demand, repeat perception and real solve times remain measurement gates.
